## Lifestyle changes
	- Reducing stress
	- Exercise
	- Meditation, prayer, or yoga
	- Avoiding stimulants and liquor
## Supplements to reduce cravings
	- N-Acetylcysteine 500 - 1,000 mg
	- Butyrate
	- Curcumin (theracumin or longivda)
	- CDP-Choline 100 - 300 mg
	- Memantine 1 -5 mg
## For withdrawal
	- L-Theanine
	- Magnesium
	- Niacin
	- Agmatine

## Additional info
	- Could pick one to three compounds to reduce cravings, they make nicotine less pleasurable
	- Taper down from whole pack to half pack, to three. Cigs
	- Get ones with less fillers
	- No need of pressure
	- NAC may cause stomach issues: insomnia, depression, or anhedonia in some people
	- Butyrate supplements can reactivate cold sores and herpes for many
	- Circumin can cause anxiety for some people, or feeling restless
	- CDP-Choline might make some people feel depressed, or anxious
	- Look into other side effects, look into any interactions and ask your doctor if it's okay to take them if you're on meds or have health conditions
