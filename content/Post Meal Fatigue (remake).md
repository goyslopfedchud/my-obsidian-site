## Ways to improve it
	- Smaller meals spread out
	- Eat protein & fiber first
	- Don't overdo fats & carbs in one large meal
	- Switch to less inflammatory carbs like white rice, sweet potato, or fruits
	- Get more sleep in general, take naps
	- Vitamin B1 50 - 100 mg, in the morning
	- Niacin 50 - 250 mg, in the morning
	- Omega 3, right before meal
	- Digestive enzymes, right before meal
	- Forskolin, in the morning

## Additional info
	- If you feel sleepy and tired after a meal, it's usually blood sugar and insulin thing; your body handles carbs poorly or you're just eating too many at once 
	- Glucose also suppresses orexin which is a neuropeptide that keeps you awake so it's like having excessive daytime sleepiness and this might be even worse if you're eating high lectin carbs probably also cause brain fog 
	- Whereas you might be okay eating sweet potato, fruits or white rice 
	- Vitamin B1 helps to no feel sleepy after carbs 
	- For some people if they overdo fats in a single meal that can release a hormone called CCK, which makes you sleepy and gassy 
	- Overdoing certain carbs or fats in a single meal that tends to just cause tiredness that's isolated to a few hours 
	- There's also foods that can cause multi day sluggishness
