
## What to do
	- Avoid stimulants and excessive caffeine
	- Hydration, avoid junk foods, light frequent meals, rest, try to reduce external stress
	- Niacin 100 mg x 2 (delirium, craving, tremor, psychosis)
	- Vitamin B1 50 mg (memory loss, fatigue)
	- Magnesium 50 mg x 3 (tremor, anxiety, sleep)
	- L-Taurine (psychosis, sleep)
	- NAC 500 - 1,000 mg (cravings & liver support)
	- Curcumin (cravings, brain & liver support)
	- Vitamin C + Activated Charcoal (reduce damage)
	- Ginger + peppermint (nausea)
	- Memantine 1 - 5 mg (anxiety)
	- Benzos or Baclofen (under doctor's supervision)

## Additional info
	- Taper because of a risk of seizure, reducing one drink every second or third day
	- Niacin can cause uncomfortable flushing, so start with 25 mg (break 100 mg tab into quarter)
	- Vitamin B1 can cause mild shock reaction at first, so do the same 
	- Make sure to look into the side effects of everything that's listed before trying any
	- The GABAergic med like benzos and baclofen should be reserved for later down the road in your tapering, not immediately, under doctor's guidance
	- Seek out medical supervision if possible
