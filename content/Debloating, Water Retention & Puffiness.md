## What makes it worse
	- Poor sleep, excess body fat, cortisol & estrogen too high, insulin resistance 
	- High histamine foods (processed meat & seafood, and fermented foods)
	- High glycemic foods & high PUFA seed oils
	- MSG, alcohol
	- NSAIDs, corticosteroids, calcium channel blockers
	- Gabapentin, diabetic meds
## What may help
	- Gus sha, Ice mask, exercise
	- Potassium or coconut water
	- Magnesium 50 - 200 mg
	- Vitamin B1 25 - 200 mg
## Diuretics
	- Dandelion
	- Stinging nettle

## Additional info
	- Edema, water retention and puffiness 
	- If there's insulin resistance, that could worsen it, most people have insulin resistance 
	- If there's excess cortisol, that might look like fat round face 
	- Histamine is a big culprit, and possibly mold 
	- Could be luteal phase, premenstrual or hypothyroid 
	- Salt itself isn't a big issue unless it's coupled with processed foods, insulin resistance and high histamine foods 
	- Being potassium deficient 
	- There's only so much supplements can do in this case, so the best bet is to avoid thing that are making you retain water
