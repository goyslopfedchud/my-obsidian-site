## Symptoms
	- Neuropathy
	- Tingling
	- Burning
	- Numbness
	- Tinnitus
	- Photophobia
	- Frequent urination
	- Insomnia
	- POTS
	- Fatigue
	- Anxiety
	- Brain fog
## Foods to avoid
	- High oxalates & high histamine foods
	- Folic acid enriched flours
	- Alcohol
## Supplements to avoid
	- Thiamine disulfides (TTFD, sulbutiamine)
	- Synthetic folic acid
	- Iron & copper
## What may help
	- Biotin 1,000 - 5,000 mcg
	- Vitamin B5 50 - 100 mg
## Others
	- Riboflavin
	- Benfotiamine
	- Folinic acid
	- Magnesium
	- Potassium
	- Molybdenum
	- Zinc

## Additional info
	- Vitamin B6 can be toxic in high doses, causing nerve irritation 
	- If you do it for too long, it leads to nerve damage and that can mimic POTS, you could get horrific insomnia from it, and neuropathic pain
	- In studies B6 is showing toxicity at hundreds of milligrams, but some people get toxic even from foods that are high in B6- Rare; if that happens B6 is not the necessary one to blame alone, you likely have high levels of heavy metal toxicity and oxalate storage and B6 works as an agitator of those 
	- If the toxicity is in the acute phase, you can use biotin and B5 to reduce the wear and tear from the circulating B6
	- When you get to the nerve damage phase, it's gonna take a lot longer to heal- For most people, around one to two years; so if it gets there the protocol changes a bit
