## Symptoms
	- Chronic fatigue
	- Asthma or other respiratory issuees
	- Eye irritation
	- Sneezing
	- Puffiness
	- Muscle and joint pain and stiffness
	- Migraines
	- CIRS
	- Brain fog
	- Anxiety
	- Depression
	- Irritability
	- Psychosis
	- Hallucination
## Lifestyle changes
	- Sauna, Epsom salt baths, relocated if possible
	- Eliminate sugar and high histamine foods
	- High quality air purifier and dehumidifier
## What may help
	- Activated Charcoal, Glucomannan, Bentonite Clay, Milk Thistle, Cholestyramine
	- Vitamin C, Glutathione or NAC, Quercetin

## Additional info
	- Having mold can worsen existing health conditions- Allergies 
	- Sometimes people move out of a moly house and their systemic illness goes into remission
	- There are a few types of mold that are just non negotiable, and there's other types of molds that you can try to build resilience against 
	- Look for MCAS and histamine; may help to reduce reaction to mold
