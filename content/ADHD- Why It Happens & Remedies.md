## Associated symptoms
	1. Lack of motivation & initiative
	2. Inattention, easily distracted
	3. Low mental energy
	4. Mood swings: low mood to mood elevation
	5. Obsessive defiance & behavioral issues
	6. Hyperactivity
	7. Restless, can't sit still
	8. Forgetful, losing things
	9. Poor impulse control
	10. Poor memory recall
	11. Excessive talking, interrupting
	12. Oversharing
	13. Irritable, aggressive
	- People with ADHD almost always has either bipolar or mood swings and vice versa, but this often gets overlooked and that might be part of the issue with psychiatry
	- You can go to different psychiatrists, present the exact same symptoms and get different diagnosis from each one of them 
## Positive charactereistics
	1. Selectively hyperfocused on projects & passions
	2. Artistic, open minded and curious
	3. More likely to be generalists
	4. Crisis induced hyperfunction
	5. Innovative & creative
	6. Empathy & humor
	7. Possibly strong leadership quality
	- The positive characteristics prevail if you're physically healthy and emotionally well off
	- Crisis induced hyperfunction- The worse it gets for everyone else, the higher functioning you become 
	- The traits we have today is because they were passed on- There had always been cataclysms resets and famines etc, thus there had to be certain percentage of the population that would thrive in that kind of chaos
	- People really wouldn't have had true ADHD before immunizations came; although neruodivergence always existed, probably more as a positive expression compared to what we have these days
## Lifestyle changes
	1. Reduce screen time (video games, doom scrolling), try not to use phone in the morning except for work
	2. Prioritize and fix sleep
	3. Get more sunlight
	4. Exercise everyday, even just a little
	5. Light meditation, yoga, or prayers when you wake up, 5 - 10 mins
	6. Avoid alcohol, or limit to one drink at a time, don't abuse stimulants
	7. Avoid using cannabis during the day
	8. Sleep aids: Z-drugs (zopiclone & zolpidem), and 2nd gen antipsychotics (seroquel & olanzapine) can cause next day fatigue and laziness
	9. Avoid overtraining, hitting failure too often
	- This is an attempt at positively rewiring you brain, we want to incorporate good habits
	- Picking up the phone first in the morning can set a lazy tone for the day 
	- Drinking alcohol may make you unmotivated for few days
	- Lack of sleep is the worst
	- Endorphine and opioid receptor; as important as dopamine receptor in the case of ADHD
## Foods to try to avoid
	1. Gluten
	2. Dairy
	3. Processed foods
	4. Refined sugar
	5. Synthetic & artificial colours, preservatives, and flavours
	- Avoiding gluten and dairy is to eliminate opioid receptor disruption, not having to use opioids
	- If you're not comfortable doing gluten and dairy free then you can at least cut out the junk foods: pizza pops, coca cola, fruit loops, and cereals- They do affect the mind a lot, you could even say they're weaponized food industry system

## Contributing & coexisting factors
	1. Junk processed foods and synthetic additives
	2. Gut microbiome imbalance
	3. Lack of sleep
	4. Excess screen time (doom scrolling, video games, netflix)
	5. Injury from childhood immunizations
	6. Nutritional deficiencies
	7. Copper / mercury / lead toxicity
	8. Alcohol & other substance use
	9. Catecholamine dysregulation (DRD4, DAT1)
	10. Low endorphins & enkephalins
	11. GABA / Glutamate imbalance
	12. Serotonin dysregulation (5HT1A/2A/2C, SLC6A4 variants)
	13. Mast cell activation
	14. Acetylcholine imbalance
	15. HPA axis dysregulation
	16. Mitochondrial dysfunction
	17. Autoimmune / post infection, affecting the basal ganglia
	18. Elevated cytokines (IL-6, TNF-a)
