## Dietary Changes
	- Intermittent fasting ( 4 - 8 hour eating window)
	- Avoid high PUFA oils ( seed oils)
	- Reduce high glycemic foods ( bread, potato)
	- Reduce sweets ( soft drinks, pastries, candies)
	- Alcohol avoidance
	- Ketogenic (optional for most)
