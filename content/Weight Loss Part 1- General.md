
## Dietary Changes
	- Intermittent fasting (4 - 8 hour eating window)
	- Avoid high PUFA oils (seed oils)
	- Reduce high glycemic foods (bread, potato)
	- Reduce sweets (soft drinks, pastries, candies)
	- Alcohol avoidance
	- Ketogenic (optional for most)
## To reduce appetite & overeating ( pick up to 3)
	- Biotin 1,000 - 3,000 mcg
	- DL-Phenylalanine 500 - 1,500 mg
	- Butyrate 250 - 1,000 mg
	- Lactoferrin (for sugar craving)
	- Hops extract
	- Spirulina 3 - 10 grams
	- Saffron extract (caution for mania)
	- Stimulants (not recommended)

## Additional info
	- This is to optimize fat loss by addressing what's hindering the metabolism
	- Throw out the industrial vegetable oils regardless of which practice you're to choose
	- If you go on a ketogenic diet, that would produce faster results: at best up to 2 kg per week
	- Some people not being able to lose weight despite caloric deficit and exercising is because of the metabolic issues: this list does address most of it
	- Biotin may help reduce inappropriate and unnecessary hunger signals
	- Phenylalanine should be taken on empty stomach (on if you don't have PKU)
	- Butyrate can cause cold sore or genital herpes breakout, if you're prone
	- Lactoferrin may reduce sugar cravings and stomach bloating
	- Hops might be a decent substitute for Metformin
	- Spirulina oddly keeps you satiated
	- Saffron is great overall, but some people experience hypomania and insomnia from it
	- Before trying anything, it is good to consult your doctor: if you have any conditions, make sure there's no interaction between that and the diet or the supplements
