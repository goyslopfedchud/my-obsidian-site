## Potential Triggers
	- Mold, pathogens, vaccines, excess wireless emissions, perfumes & scents, stress
	- Gluten, high salicylate & histamine foods, alcohol
	- Excess B12 or iodine, niacin, some probiotics, carnosine, histidine, essential oils
## What may help
	- Quercetin, EGCG, or Luteolin
	- Zinc 5 - 15 mg
	- Vitamin C 500 - 2,000 mg
	- Vitamin B1 25 - 100 mg
	- Molybdenum 100 - 250 mcg
	- Others:
	- Controlled sun exposure
	- CBD oil
	- Palmitoylethanolamide
	- LDN 0.25 - 4.5 mg

	- The mast cells overreact and release histamine and other inflammatory mediators whereas other this is a protective mechanism 
	- You wanna try to minimize the trigger first so you can stabilize it a bit, most importantly gluten, histamine, and processed foods in general
	- Mold is almost inevitable :( we can become more resilient to it though
	- Once you're stable enough to do supplements, recommend low and gradual vitamin B1 and then zinc with zinc ionophores like quercetin, EGCG, or lutelion; after that you might be able to reintroduce some foods
