
## Dietary changes
	- Avoid high PUFA oils
	- Low glycemic diet
## To manage blood glucose (pick up to 3)
	- Benfotiamine 100 - 600 mg
	- Sodium R-Lipoate 100 - 300 mg
	- Chromium 200 - 400 mcg
	- Vanadium
	- Hops
	- Bitter melon
	- Berberine
	- Cinnamon
	- Metformin

## Additional info
	- Symptom wise hyperglycemia generally present as excessive thirst, tiredness, and sleepiness after meal, frequent urination, possibly mood swings; can happen if you're on certain meds like antipsychotics, maybe blood pressure meds and beta blockers
	- If you drink a lot then pancreatitis, you might just be more prone to hyperglycemia possibly due to autoimmune issues
	- Keto and intermittent fasting recommended, but it carries risk for ketoacidosis and hypoglycemia especially for diabetics
	- If you're type 1 diabetic then don't do any of this, instead, go gluten free and grain free-paleo diet, then you can have berries and sweet potatoes
	- Type 1 is autoimmune based so you don't want to consume anything that your body might recognize as foreign substance
	- Soium R-Lipoate, aka Alpha Lipoic Acid, should be looked into first if you take 1st or 2nd gen antipsychotic, benfotiamine (or Vitamin B1) might be an easier and safer option to start with
	- Some people cannot lose weight just by going caloric deficit or by exercising; insulin is one of the fators
	- If you get hypoglycemic then you will need to be more careful about lowering blood glucose with supplements
	- It is helpful to have blood glucose testing kit, you may see that eating gluten and wheat flours raise blood glucose to unreasonable levels compared to other starchy foods
	- The high PUFA oils, aka seed oils, such as canola, peanut, soy, sesame, grapeseed, corn, cottonseed oil, can be switched to grass fed butter, olive oil, coconut oil, or avocado oil
	- Fruits and pure maple syrup are preferred over highly processed sweets with artificial colours and flavours like fruit loops and most cereals
	- Before trying any supplements, make sure you look into interaction with your meds or conditions
