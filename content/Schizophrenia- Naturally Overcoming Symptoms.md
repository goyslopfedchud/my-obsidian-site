## Supplements to avoid
	1. Copper
	2. Iron
	3. Folic acid 
	- Copper may contribute to hallucinations
	- Iron likely contributes to mania, racing thoughts 
	- Folic acid seems to make people crazy
	- Follic, not methylfolate or folinic acid
## Deficiencies to correct
	1. Niacin / niacinamide 50 - 300 mg
	2. Folinic acid / methylfolate 200 mcg
	3. Vitamin B1 25 - 200 mg
	4. Molybdenum 100 - 250 mcg
	5. Lithium orotate 1 - 5 mg
	- Start with the deficiencies first
	- Niacin should be given in mental hospitals or at least be recommended during an active schizo episodes alongside with antipsychotics
	- If you take too much niacin for too long that can turn into hypomania 
	- For niacin, start at 50 and build up to 300 mg, then as a maintenance dose, just wanna do 100 mg a day- A good long term maintenance dose
	- Folinic acid and methylfolate could be what gives the effect of snapping you back into reality
	- Folinic acid / methylfolate usually come as 1 mg but that may cause side effects such as anger or insomnia- Stick around 200 to 250 micrograms per day by splitting the tablets
	- The deficiency of vitamin B1 can resemble psychosis; side effects like anger, insomina, worsening psychosis if taken too much from the beginning
