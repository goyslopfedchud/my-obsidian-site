## What makes it worse
	- Gluten
	- High lectin foods (whole grains, nuts, beans, legumes, lentils, soy)
## What might help
	- Biotin 500 - 20,000 mcg
	- Vitamin B1 25 - 200 mg
	- L-Carnitine 100 - 500 mg
	- Forskolin 
	- Fermented foods (in moderation)
## Medications
	- Pitolisant
	- Xyrem
	- Modafinil
