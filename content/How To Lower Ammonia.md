Common symptoms of hyperammonemia
brain fog, anxiety, irritability, low attention span, insomnia, fatigue, nausea, vomiting, stomach pains, mood swings, psychosis, excessive daytime sleepiness
What raises ammonia
	- Excess protein, alcohol, sugar, valproic acid
	- Weak liver or kidney, urea cycle disorders (UCD)
	- Overgrowth of bad gut bacteria, parasites
Supplements
	- L-Mrnithine 200 - 500 mg (not if you have UCD)
	- Oxaloacetate, or malic acid
	- Manganese 5 mg
	- L-Arginine ( not if you have herpes)
	- L-Citrulline
	- Activated Charcoal
	- Zinc

Additional info
	- Ammonia is highly neurotoxic and our body produces it 
	- Ammonia gets elevated usually because of the liver or the kidneys are struggling- there could be parasites, it could just be bad gut bacteria that's producing more ammonia; Candida
	- Some people eat unreasonable amount of protein, sugar, fructose, alcohol, too much steroids
	- For hyperammonemia: blood ammonia test, kidney function test, CBS mutation (genetic test), brain MRI (for brain swelling)
