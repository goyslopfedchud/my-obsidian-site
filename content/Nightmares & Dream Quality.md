## What may cause nightmares
	- Laying on your back during sleep
	- Poor sleep schedule, horror movies
	- Eating too close to bedtime, especially protein, dairy, and spicy foods
	- Alcohol, TCAs, SSRIs, Z-Drugs, Beta Blocker
## For dream quality
	- Biotin 500 - 3,000 mcg (for nightmares)
	- Vitamin B6 5- 10 mg (for dream recall)
