## What may worsen symptoms
	- Excessive digital entertainment and scorlling
	- Alcohol (limit to one drink per session)
	- Gluten, sugar, and processed foods
	- Nutrient deficiencies
	- Immunizations, COVID shots
	- Poor sleep, snoring & sleep apnea
	- Antipsychotics used as sleep aid
## What may improve symptoms
	- Regular exercise, sunlight in the morning
	- Meditation, yoga, prayer, staring at a wall
	- Paleo or ketogenic diet, if possible
	- Leaving the phone in another room
	- Reading, writing, creative projects
	- Taking a nap

## Additional info
	- Excessive entertainment on screen like netflix and video games can tank your attention span and motivation, same with alcohol
	- Diet, going sugar free is a good start
