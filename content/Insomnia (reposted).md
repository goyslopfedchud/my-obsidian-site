## Things That May Cause Sleep Issues
	- Minor insomnia (usually easy to manage)
		- Stimulants, drinking frequently
		- Junk foods, gluten & dairy, excess sugar
		- Solution: caffeine, coenzyme Q10, vitamin B12, NAC, omega 3, vitamin D3, zinc, vitamin K, glycine
	- Dragon Level Insomnia
		- Iron supplements of toxicity
		- Vitamin B6 toxicity
		- High dose niacin
		- Retinoids, accutane
	- God level insomnia
		- Over supplementing copper or toxicity
		- Over supplementing iodine / hyperthyroidism
		- Benzo or Gabapentin withdrawal 
		- Opioid withdrawal
## Additional info
	- Not medical advice
	- Sleep hygiene is good to do, although not everyone has the time for it and it doesn't work for everyone
	- Melatonin is to be taken as you're about to lay down to reduce norepinephrine 
	- Magnesium is to block the NMDA receptor to help sleep onset and staying asleep 
	- Phosphatidylserine might make you feel like going to bed and helps to blunt the cortisol spike so you don't randomly wake up 
	- Any one of these here can paradoxically worsen sleep; it's rare and just a matter of compatibility
	- There is extremely rare cases where none of these work: taking 7 different meds and 8 different supplements to sleep 2 hours per night- cases like this would be some kind of toxicity
