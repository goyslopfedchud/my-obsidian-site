
## Benefits& upsides:
	- COX inhibition - Lowering prostaglandins
	- Increases tyrosine hydroxylase
	- May protect dopamine neurons
	- Anti cortisol, serotonin, and aromatase
	- Helpful in preventing heart attacks
	- May reduce damage from iron toxicity
	- May improve erectile dysfunciton
## Side effects & downsides to look into
	- Tinnitus, intestinal permeability, bleeding, bruising, blood thinning, Reye's, salicylate intolerance
## Dosage: 80 - 500 mg
## Frequent users may consider taking vitamin K2

	- Improved cognition and clearance of brain fog
	- Anti cancer, anti estrogen, anti serotonin
	- Mitochondrial support
	- Improved sleep
	- Relieving frequent urination / overactive bladder
	- Protecting arteries from calcification
