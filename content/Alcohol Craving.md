## What may increase cravings
	- Trazodone or antipsychotics used as sleep aids, stimulants
	- Poor diet and high sugar intake
## What may reduce cravings
	- Ketogenic diet
	- NAC 500 - 1,000 mg
	- Curcumin (theracumin or longvida)
	- Butyrate
	- Niacin 50 - 250 mg
	- L-Glutamine 500 - 2,000 mg
## Apollo Pharmakeia
	- Topiramate
	- Memantine
	- Naltrexone
	- Acamprosate
