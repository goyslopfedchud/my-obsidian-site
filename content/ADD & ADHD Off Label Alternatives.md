## Dopaminergic meds (one at a time)
	- Bupropion ( Wellbutrin)
	- Modafinil or Armodafinil 25 - 200 mg
	- Amantadine 25 - 50 mg, up to 3x a day
	- Bromantane 50 - 200 mg 
	- Selegiline 2.5 - 5 mg, up to 2x a day
	- Rasagiline 0.25 - 1 mg
## Others
	- LDN (Low Dose Naltrexone) 0.25 - 4.5 mg
	- Racetams ( Piracetam, Aniracetam)
	- Sulbutiamine 100 - 300 mg, occasionally

## Additional info
	- General google search things: dexies, addies, alpha 2 agonists, tricyclics, atomoxetine
	- Dopaminergic meds: get prescribed one at a time; no mixing unless you know what you're doing 
	- Amphetties raise dopamine as well as endorphins and enkephalins 
	- This is not a medical advice: do own research and go through with doctor's approval and the proper steps to getting prescribed
	- Almost all of the dopaminergic listed have potential side effects like anxiety, irritability, and mood changes, headache, insomnia, tachycardia and high BP, and seizure
	- Bupropion and Selegiline carries the risk of discontinuation syndrome
	- The racetams and sulbutiamine may help with cognition and memory but task focus and motivation are not particularly their strong points
