
## Main symptoms
	1. Fatigue not relieved by rest
	2. PEM (nausea, brain fog, dizziness, and pain after exercise)
	3. Orthostatic intolerance
	4. Brain fog & cognitive impairment
## Other symptoms
	1. Autonomic nervous system dysfunction
	2. Chronic unexplained pains: muscles & joints, neck, throat, chest
	3. Neuropathic pains or numbness
	4. Sensitive to noise, light, pain, and chemicals
	5. MCAS, histamine intolerance
	6. Interstitial cystitis, frequent urination
	7. Flu-like symptoms
	8. Irritable bowel
	9. Sensitivity to food, medications and supplements
	10. Depression, mood swings, anxiety, feeling out of touch with reality
	11. Problems with word recall, and stuttering
	12. Difficulty regulating body temperature, sensitive to cold and heat
## Lifestyle changes
	1. Avoid gluten, grains, dairy, high histamine & high oxalate foods, nuts & seeds, high PUFA seed oils
	2. Avoid exerting beyond capacity
	3. Fix sleep & address sleep apnea
	4. Reduce heavy metal & EMF exposure if possible
	5. Getting more sunlight
	6. Eliminate mold from the environment if possible, if not then high quality air purifier and dehumidifier
	7. Don't overdo stimulants, avoid alcohol
	8. Safe removal of amalgams

## Additional info
	- This does apply to post vaccine, post viran and long covid
	- CFS is complex multisystem disorder: patient could have 50 symptoms or more, and that doesn't happen with most other conditions, making CFS one of the worst acquired conditions you can get, also the hardest to treat, nearly impossible to go into remission from 
	- To minimize the inflammation and autoimmunity we want to avoid foods like gluten, grains, and dairy 
	- You'll probably get withdrawals from food, because foods are very addictive 
	- Is the diet negotiable?- Leaning towards no because it's multi system complex disorder including autoimmunity
	- If the number of symptoms and their severity is on the lower side then you could just eliminate processed foods and junk foods and still have the bread if you must rather than strict diet 
	- It is notable that CFS is unlike any other disorders that have one big culprit that can be addressed; but CFS basically being an attack from all angles
