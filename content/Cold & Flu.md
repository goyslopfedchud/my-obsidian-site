## Lifestyle changes
	- Get more sunlight, reduce stress & EMF exposure, adequate sleep & protein shake
	- Avoid dairy, sugar, processed foods and alcohol especially when sick 
## Supplements
	- Zinc 5 - 15 mg
	- Quercetin / EGCG / HCQ
	- Vitamin C 500 - 1,000 mg
	- Palmitoylethanolamide 300 - 600 mg
	- NAC 600 mg
## When you're about to get a cold or flu
	- Elderberry
	- Andrographis
	- Bee Propolis
	- Oregano Oil

## Additional info
	- To prevent cold & flu, and many other viral infections
	- It is a lot easier to prevent it or to stop it in its tracks before they get serious 
	- Combining low dose zinc with quercetin, a zing ionophore, helps zinc get in the cell, or EGCG or hydroxychloroquine are good substitute for quercetin, taking them during the cold and flu seasons 
	- If you feel a cold coming on, you can double the dose and take things like elderberry and you can do intermittent fasting or just one meal a day with no dairy and sugar and NAC to help with the mucus
