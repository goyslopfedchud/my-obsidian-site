## What to avoid
	1. Nuts (almonds, peanuts, hazelnuts, macadamia, cashew, walnuts)
	2. Seeds (sesame, sunflower, pumpkin)
	3. Coconut, coconut flout, coconut milk, coconut butter
	4. Soy products (tofu, saitan, tempeh, natto)
	5. Oats and whole grains
	6. Chocolates
	7. Arginine
	8. Citrus foods
	9. Hot spices (cayenne, chili, ghost)
	10. Forskolin
	11. Butyrate
	12. Excessive sunlight
	- Avoid especially the top 6; those are high in amino acids called arginine 
	- The herpes virus requires arginine for its growth and to replicate
	- Some people take lysine which is antagonistic to arginine in this instance- With the top three foods like nuts, seeds, and coconuts, they have such a high arginine content that lysine just doesn't seem to do the job, and citrus foods like tomatoes can be an issue, although it doesn't usually really cause severe breakouts, high arginine foods talked here would be like, they'll cause seriously big breakouts on the outer areas of the lips- Very visible, very big
	- Hot spices might cause issues; like a small sore on the lip that goes away fairly quickly whereas high arginine foods tend to last a week or two and as soon as they're healed, another one comes back
	- Forskolin and butyrate can be an issue, as forskolin can reactivate some neurons where the herpes viruses are dormant, and butyrate kind of works similarly to arginine, encouraging the replications of the viruses, but usually not to the same extent as arginine
## Supplements
	1. Lysine
	2. Monolaurin
	3. Butylated Hydroxytoluene (BHT)
	4. Silver (colloidal / ionic)
	5. Sulforaphane (broccoli sprouts)
	6. Resveratrol
	7. Olive Leaf Extract
	8. Curcumin
	9. Reishi
	10. Quercetin
	- These all have been shown to either inhibit the herpes virus or reduce the reoccurrence of breakouts
	- Don't go in depth with all the supplements 
	- Lysine is going to be your standard go-to, it is an amino acid, so it's unlikely that you're going to have much negatives reactions to it
	- Monolourin is also quite friendly; even if you have autoimmune issues, is generally well tolerated and the only possible time it might not do so well for you is if you have like high load of pathogens in the system
	- The BHT, butylated hydroxytoluene, it's understandable that it's seen as a toxic compound, but also there are people with herpes that have taken a whole gram per day for years with no adverse effects, and it's probably going to be the most effective one in this list
	- Silver is generally okay, keep it under one teaspoon per day, which is no more than 30 parts per million 
	- Sulforophane can be too stimulating for some, but it is extremely effective 
	- Resveratrol, olive leaf extract, curcumin, reishi and quercetin, use them as personal preference, going out of your way to get them is not recommended, unless you had good reactions to them in the past, then by all means they are very effective
	- If you want to get started with supplements, lysine and BHT if you feel comfortable with it, are recommended; otherwise, monolaruin and silver, just don't overdo silver
