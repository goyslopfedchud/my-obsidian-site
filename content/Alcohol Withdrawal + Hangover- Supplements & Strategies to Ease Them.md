
## Supplements
	1. Combo for anxiety and hostility
		- L-Orithine 400 mg
		- Ashwagandha
		- Shilajit
	2. For Nausea
		- Ginger
		- Peppermint
	3. General
		- Niacin 50 - 500 mg (shaking, anger, craving, delirium & psychosis)
		- Vitamin B1 25 - 100 mg (memory loss, anger & fatigue)
		- L-Taurine 500 mg (for psychosis)
		- NAC (liver support & cravings)
		- Zinc 5 - 15 mg (general deficiency)
		- Magnesium 50 - 200 mg (shaking, insomnia & fatigue)
	- You start trying to acquire these supplements and meds as soon as possible, get in touch with the doctor, get the necessary meds- Recommended method of withdrawal
	- First three help with anxiety and hostility induced by hangover
	- Grind up some ginger, drink some peppermint tea, or sniff some peppermint essential oils for nausea
	- If you're getting delirium & psychosis you should be seeking medical help 
	- Niacin is important; it is recommended to collect most of the supplements listed here but if you're not able to all, make sure niacin's collected, especially if you've been continuously drinking for like over a week or a month
	- Niacin can help with one of nasty terminal symptoms, thus it will make you flush and itchy which is normal, making sure not taking too much since it can come with its own issues, 500 mg a day max
	- Alcohol depletes thiamine (B1) a lot, so you get acute deficiency, where if it goes long enough getting long term memory loss; start at 25 mg
	- Taurine might help with psychosis 
	- NAC can make drinking less pleasurable, might give you the sense of clarity, so you're not compulsively drinking 
	- Don't exceed 15 mg of zinc on its own
	- Magnesium is great especially while you're tapering, preferably 50 mg few times a day 

## Medications
	1. Under supervision
		- Gabapentin
		- Benzos
		- Memantine
		- Phenibut
		- Baclofen
		- Barbiturates
		- Beta blockers
	- You preferably start medications after tapering 
	- E.g.: you safely got down to three drinks per day, and you're ready to jump, then you may get these prescribed 
	- If you take them while you're tapering, that can create more problems later on 
	- Mematine is the least problematic one in the list
## Neurochemical & Metabolic drivers
	1. GABA downregulation
	2. Glutamate upregulation
	3. 5HT2A upregulation
	4. Ketoacidosis
	5. Hypoglycemia
	- GABA downregulation and glutamate upregulation would look like anxiety and shaking, possibly hallucination, can be addressed with the medications 
	- 5HT2A upregulation so serotonin 2A receptor, would look like hallucination, delirium, psychosis; niacin might address this, as well as antipsychotics, which can bring problems of its own 
	- Ketoacidosis has like fruity breath, smelling like nail polish remover, smelling smoky: vitamin B1 and quarter teaspoon of baking soda for this
	- You can do slow reiintroduction of carbs for hypoglycemia 
## Medical Complications
	1. Hallucination & psychosis
	2. Seizure
	3. Delirium tremens
	4. Insomnia
	- Meds like trazodone, zopiclone, seroquel, olanzapine, cyproheptadine, can increase relapse rate if you use those as sleep meds
## Common Mistakes
	1. Quitting cold turkey with no support
	2. Using stimulants to push through
	3. Certain sleep med increase relapse rates
## Preferred lifestyle changes
	1. Hydration + electrolytes
	2. Supervised tapering
	3. Low stress environment & time off
	4. Regular balanced meals
## When to seek emergency care
	1. High fever
	2. Severe agitation
	3. Seizure
	4. Persistent vomiting
