
## Meds to help with taper (under supervision)
	- Topiramate
	- Memantine 2.5 - 5 mg (NMDA antagonist)
	- Benzos, low dose (GABA-A agonist)
	- Baclofen 2.5 - 10 mg (GABA-B agonist)
## For Sleep
	Melatonin 0.5 mg, Valerian, Lemon Balm, L-Theanine
	DORA: Lemborexant or Suvorexant
## Neuropathic pains
	Hydroxocobalamin 500 mcg, Benfotiamine 300 mg, Palmitoylethanolamide 600 mg, LDN 0.25 - 4.5 mg
## Anxiety & Mood swings
	Taurine, NAC, Agmatine(if memantine not available)

## Additional info
	- Gabapentin works through alpha 2 delta subunit to reduce calcium influx into the neuron, so when you stop you get a surge of glutamate and norepinephrine thus not a direct GABA agonist
	- Gabapentinoids misuse is somewhat common, possibly 40 - 65% of the people with valid prescriptions are misusing gabapentin or pregabalin
	- It's addictive, and tolerance builds surprisingly quick, this one has particularly low fun to withdrawal ratio
	- For tapering, you can cut the tables or get an empty capsule to divide the dosage, or mix it in liquid to measure the dose
	- If there's history of seizure, look into eliminating gluten, MSG, and aspartame from the diet, during the taper
	- If you decide to go with benzos make sure to get it through prescription, low dosage, only take when needed during taper, no more than once a day; make sure it doesn't have long half life, and if you have trouble with compulsive use, then avoid this option
	- The supplements listed are from limited available evidence, some anecdotes, or copy from benzo tapering, so they might not be that effective
	- Magnesium can reduce absorption of gabapentin, so take it 2 hours away if you do
