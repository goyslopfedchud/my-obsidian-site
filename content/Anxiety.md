## Additional info
	- Try keto diet at least at some point especially if you don't want to do medications or supplements
	- If the anxiety is felt in the chest without heart issues, you can try molybdenum or L-Taurine, maybe niacinamide
	- If there's mood swings then lithium or NAC, maybe B6
	- Anger issues, then lithium or molybdenum
	- The depressive type of anxiety then B6 or Amatine, maybe low dose zinc
	- Frequent tiredness then molybdenum and B1
	- Say benzos have worked well in the past or there is trouble reading social cues or sensory overload, maybe tired but wired type of feeling, look at raising GABA; passion flower, black seed oil, L-Tuarine, kava, agmatine, memantine, baclofen
	- If it's OCD type of anxiety; NAC, fluvoxamine, maybe D-Cycloserine with the CBT
	- If it's psychosis type of anxiety like paranoia being overly spiritual, feeling like under spiritual attack; niacin, fluvoxamine, Abilify (aripiprazole), maybe taurine
