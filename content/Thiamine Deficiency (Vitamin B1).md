## Symptoms
	- Physical fatigue & weakness, exercise intolerance, loss of appetite, constipation, digestive issues, edema, shortness of breath
## Autonomic
	- Orthostatic intolerance, poor temperature regulation, lack of sweating, sleep apnea
## Cardiovascular
	- Tachycardia, low blood pressure
## Neurological
	- Neuropathy, pins & needles, urinary urgency (small bladder feeling)
## Neuropsychiatric
	- Depression, anxiety, irritability, psychosis, apathy
## Cognitive
	- Brain fog, confusion, memory impairment, poor word recall, stuttering

## Additional info
	- Vitamin B1 deficiency can mimic some conditions or worsen them and it can be the worst, has the most variations of symptoms
	- It can happen from consuming lots of sugar, carbs, alcohol, maybe antibiotics, long term stress, maybe gut issues or infections 
	- It takes time to get a reliable test, the fastest way to test deficiency is to get a thiamine hydrochloride 100 mg, which is the easiest and the cheapest form to get, with it, you're looking for that initial shock reaction, which could be irritability, anxiety, tiredness, headache, might feel extreme hunger, not sleep well
	- After the initial shock, cut back the dose and as symptoms subside each day gradually increase the dose, every week or every few days
	- Vitamin B1 is one of the very few nutrients that causes initial refeeding shock, otherwise known as paradoxical reaction
	- 100 - 200 mg of thiamine HCI on your day off, away from family and friends
	- If you're severely sick and tired all the time, then start with 25 - 50 mg
	- If taking thiamine makes you anxious, angry, tired, or get headache or insomnia, then it's a good sign of deficiency
	- Minor mistake is raising the dose too fast; bigger mistake is quitting
	- Example: If 100 mg causes somwhat harsh reaciton, you can cut back to 50 mg a day, then after a week, try going back on 100 mg
	- Things to help with paradoxical reacitons: 
	- Magnesium 100 - 400 mg
	- Potassium 400 - 1,000 mg or coconut water
	- Biotin 1,0000 mcg
	- Molybdenum 100 - 250 mcg
