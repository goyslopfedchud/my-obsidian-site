	- tachycardia, low blood pressure
## Neurological
	- neuropathy, pins & needles, urinary urgency ( small bladder feeling)
## Neuropsychiatric
	- depression, anxiety, irritability, psychosis, apathy
## Cognitive
	- brain fog, confusion, memory impariment, poor word recall, sttutering

## Additional info
	- Vitamin B1 deficiency can mimic some conditions or worsen them and it can be the worst, has the most variations of symptoms
	- It can happen from consuming lots of sugar, carbs, alcohol, maybe antibiotics, long term stress, maybe gut issues or infections 
	- It takes time to get a reliable test, the fastest way to test deficiency is to get a thiamine hydrochloride 100 mg, which is the easiest and the cheapest form to get, with it, you're looking for that initial shock reaction, which could be irritability, anxiety, tiredness, headache, might feel extreme hunger, not sleep well
	- After the initial shock, cut back the dose and as symptoms subside each day gradually increase the dose, every week or every few days
