## Lifestyle changes
	- Keep a food journal and record how you feel
	- Eliminate gluten, dairy, alcohol, processed foods, and artificial foods
	- Reduce grains, sugar, and high FODAMAP foods
## What may help
	- Lactoferrin 250 - 500 mg
	- Hydroxocobalamin 250 mcg lozenge, or injection
	- Vitamin B1 50 - 250
	- Butyrate
	- Molybdenum 100 - 259 mcg
	- Betaine HCI (with meals)
	- Bile salts (with meals)
	- Digestive enzyme (with meals)

## Additional info
	- There's lots of success stories you can find with B1, deficiency can weaken your ability to digest foods and cause motility issues
	- Lactoferrin is anti bloating, anti gas, helps with the bacteria part as well
	- Molybdenum can help with the sulfur gas and the reflux
	- Playing cat and mouse with the supplements might not fix it all the way
	- The whole thing with SIBO testing and diagnosis was a bit misleading, in a way of just blaming bacteria, which was very profitable 
	- There is obviously struggling digestive system and presence of foods that our body does not like 
	- Changing what you eat is encouraged 
	- Elemental diet and carnivore diet are very difficult, and most people with SIBO won't have to go that far
	- Eliminating gluten isn't a big task in comparison; it won't be a cure on its own but a necessary catalyst
	- Look into probiotics, low CFU ones
	- Betaine HCI and digestive enzymes may cause stomach pains
	- Antibiotic approach feels like throwing a grenade without properly assessing what's on the other side
