## Antagonists
	- Lemborexant (Dayvigo) 17 hours half life
	- Suvorexant (Belsomra) 12 hours
	- Daridorexant (Quviviq) 8 hours
## Pros
	- Generally no withdrawal symptoms
	- Weight neutral
## Cons
	- More expensive than other sleep med
	- Insurance companies may restrict coverage
## Side effects
	- Mild fatigue next day
	- Laziness and slow start
	- Depression
	- Nausea
	- Sleep paralysis
	- Whole body vibrating sensation
	- Anxiety if you take more than tolerated

## Additional info
	- These are sleep meds that are dual orexin antagonist, biochemically it's kind of like mimicking narcolepsy
	- The side effects of these can be tolerated better than other sleeping meds, whereas antipsychotics and zopiclone may make you very tired the next day, depressed and even irritable 
	- Zopiclone can be addictive
	- Seroquel and olanzapine gives you extreme hunger and massive weight gain 
	- Ultimately you want to fix sleep naturally, but sleep hygiene doesn't work for everyone 
	- Dayvigo is just one of the not so bad medications
