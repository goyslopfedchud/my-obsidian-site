	- Memantine 2.5 - 10 mg (anxiety, cravings)

## Caution
	- LDN: probably doesn't work well until later stages of PAWS
	- Buprenorphine: may cause precipitated withdrawal
	- Gabapentin: raises the risk of OD, don't take while still using
	- Tianeptine: possible questionable safety while using
	- Dexedrine: might increase cravings
	- Check local laws before admitting to anything
	- Not all countries have patient to doctor confidentiality, nor good samaritan laws

## Additional info
	- You want to spread out during the day for sodium ascorbate, not taking 100 g at once, doesn't work for everyone
	- With the post acute withdrawal they don't always go away; it might not be so much to do with the damage that the drug caused you, so ask if you had those symptoms before you started using it, probably?, or at least there are a lot of abuse, depression and pains that led up to that
	- Dysfunctional opioid system makes you more susceptible to Opioid Use Disorder
	- These are not going to cure the psychological trauma but at least biochemically gives you more incentives to stay off of it 
	- You have to not let the stigma control how you feel: the guilt, shame probably makes you want to do it more, but it's just a lot easier to solve the problems when you're sober
