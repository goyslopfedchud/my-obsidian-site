## Lifestyle change
	- Getting more sun and exercise optimize sleep
	- Reduce time on cell phone and gaming
	- Reduce junk food, gluten, dairy, sugar, alcohol, lectin, histamine, and caffeine
## Supplements that may help
	- Vitamin B1 50 - 200 mg
	- Hydroxocobalamin 250 - 1,000 mg (occasionally)
	- EGCg or Lactoferrin
	- Molybdenum 250 - 500 mcg

## Additional info
	- High histamine, high cortisol, hypothyroid, issues with hypothalamus can cause brain fog- Most of these due to immunizations: people with covid shots frequently have brain fog
	- Try to optimize the gut health, see what supplement stacks works
	- Brain fog rarely comes on its own, people with brain fog usually have sleep issues, fatigue, and pains: usually things that are very hard to solve with labs or a simple medication
	- Most cases should improve a lot with lifestyle changes and correcting deficiencies in B1 & B12 (hydroxocobalamin)
	- If you feel angry or anxious often, then molybdenum
	- EGCg or Lactoferrin if you frequently gassy, not sleeping well, or apathetic
	- If tired and lightheaded, then B1 + ginseng
	- Omega 3 & CDP-Choline if there's issues with memory, but they can make some people depressed or irritable
	- Creatine might help if you have poor sleep schedule
	- NAC if there's mood swings or impulsivity
