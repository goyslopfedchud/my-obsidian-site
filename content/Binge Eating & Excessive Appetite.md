## What may increase hunger
	- Insulin & lectin resistance, frequent glucose spikes, cortisol too high
	- Not eating enough protein and fiber
	- Antipsychotics, corticosteroids, cannabis
	- Vitamin B1, digestive enzymes, zinc
## What may reduce hunger & cravings
	- Biotin 1,000 - 3,0000 mcg (proteins)
	- B6 5 - 20 mg (carbs)
	- Lactoferrin 250 - 500 mg (sweets, ice cream)
	- Vitamin C 500 mg (juice, salad)
## Others
	- Psyllium Husks
	- Chia Seeds
	- Moringa
	- Spirulina
	- D-Phenylalanine
	- Saffron

## Additional info
	- This is to reduce food cravings and excessive and frequent hunger, also very helpful to improve food discipline
	- Ideally we just stabilize the blood sugar and generally eating enough proteins, fiber does matter a lot too
	- Just know that gluten and dairy has endorphin like peptides so you're not exactly a junkie but they are addictive; then you'd look at D-phenylalanin and lactoferrin 
	- Say you eat like thousand calories in one sitting and still feel hungry after, then spirulina works well 
	- Excessive hunger doesn't mean you lack discipline
