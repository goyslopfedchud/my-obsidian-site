
## Dietary changes
	- Avoid dairy (milk & whey)
	- Reduce high glycemic foods, sugar, gluten, and alcohol
## Other triggers
	- Excess B12 & biotin, steroids, corticosteroids, iodine
	- Some contraceptives, DHEA
## Supplements that may help
	- L-Taurine 250 - 500 mg
	- Zinc 5 - 12.5 mg
	- Niacinamide 100 - 250 mg
	- Vitamin B5 100 - 250 mg
	- DIM 100 - 200 mg
	- Green Tea Extract 200 - 400 mg
## Skincare
	- Benzoyl Peroxide, sulfur, Salicylic Acid, Tea Tree Oil, Thyme Oil

## Additional info
	- The main culprit is usually dairy, mainly milk and whey whereas some cheese are okay and butter is okay too 
	- High glycemic foods can be an issue 
	- These are stimulating acne producing pathways and clogging the pores
	- Gluten is big too, but going gluten and dairy free for acne sounds a bit of an overkill for acne, but it is dose dependent, try to reduce them a bit
	- Most of supplements above are good for food induced acne and possibly PCOS acne and steroid induced acne too 
	- Taurine is probably the most versatile one, DIM is more for premenstrual and luteal phase acne
	- If you take B12 in excess that can cause acne, usually around the forehead, which you'll kind of need benzoyl peroxide, or diluted tea tree oil
	- Vitamin B5 is good if you have greasy skin, greasy hair, or let's say you get biotin induced acne, usually around the nose
