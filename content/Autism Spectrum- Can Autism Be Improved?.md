## Interventions
	1. Eliminate processed foods, gluten, dairy, grains, synthetic colours and preservatives
	2. Consider ketogenic diet
	3. Reduce high oxalate foods gradually- Facebook group: Trying Low Oxalates (TLO)
	4. More sunlight exposure
	5. Address mold issues- If not, get air purifier and dehumidifier
	6. Find ongoing infections: EBV, strep, staph, spirochete, H. Pylori, candida, parasites
	7. Avoid cooking with aluminium foils, teflon cookware, and commercial deodorant and baking powder
	- There are probably a lot of things that are outside of your reach currently, like getting tests; could be financial or time constraints, taking too long to get support from specialists 
	- Diet is something that can make a drastic difference in a matter of couple of weeks- Preferably ketogenic diet without synthetic colours and additives 
	- Ketogenic diet is anti inflammatory, autoimmune friendly, raises GABA
	- See if you can reduce aluminium exposure
	- If you absolutely can't stick to a diet, that's okay too since there's more to it and don't let this be a deal breaker for you and rationalize that this is out of bounds
