

## Possible triggers
	- Reduce sugar, caffeine, high oxalate foods, citrus fruits, cranberry juice, carbonated drinks
	- Avoid gluten, artificial sweeteners and erythritol, if possible
## What improves it
	- Vitamin B1 50 - 100 mg
	- Vitamin B5 100 - 250 mg
	- Quercetin
	- Zinc 5 - 15 mg, keep dose low
	- Lactoferrin
	- Saw Palmtto & Beta Sitosterol (BPH men)
	- D-Mannose & Cranberry juice (UTI)
	- Cannabis
	- Aspirin

## Additional info
	- It's for overactive bladder and interstitial cystitis- The urgency and small bladder feeling, getting up at night to go often 
	- Could get it from nerve damage or irritation, elevated prostaglandin, low vasopressin, low cortisol, insulin resistance, could have POTS, could be from inflamed prostate so BPH
	- The nerves of the bladder are pretty sensitive, UTI could be another cause but often times you get tested then it's not
	- Dietary oxalates are just too high, like eating too much spinach or celery, could be chocolate, beets, potatoes, which can eventually lead to kidney stones
	- Supplements that might make frequent urination worse:
	- L-Serine
	- Phosphatidylserine
	- Excess B6
	- Excess zinc
	- Iron
	- Copper
	- D-Mannose
	- Creatine
	- Cartitine
	- Try B5 for nocturia, difficulty sleeping, feeling angry 
	- B1 in general, and for IC
	- Lactoferrin if you have yellow eyes, dark circles under eyes, coughing, constipation
	- Aspirin and Canna if you can't avoid gluten
	- You can try eating more salt as well
