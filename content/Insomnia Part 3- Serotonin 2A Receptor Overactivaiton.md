## What may aggravate 5HT2A receptor
	- Chronic stress, cortisol, mold 
	- Gluten, processed foods, poor gut health
## What may help
	- More sunlight
	- Meditation / prayer
	- Inositol 500 - 3,000 mg, during the day
	- Chromium 200 - 400 mcg, during the day
	- Lithium Orotate 1 - 5 mg
	- Niacin 50 - 100 mg
	- Feverfew, occasional use
## Big Pharma
	- Cypropheptadine 0.5 - 1 mg
	- Trazodone 12.5 - 25 mg

## Additional info
	- Serotonin 2A receptor overactivation causes problem with falling asleep but you can generally stay asleep
	- Other symptoms: getting tired easily, depression, hypomania, mood swings, social anxiety, neurotic and impulsive, the thoughts can be obsessive, ruminating and paranoid, stomach problems-- Bloating and IBS, maybe prone to migraine
	- 2nd & 3rd gen antipsychotics antagonizes the serotonin 2A receptor- Seroquel and olanzapine; these make you tired the next day and prone to extreme anger
	- Hallucinations: LSD, Magic mushroom, Dimethyltryptamine activate the serotonin 2A receptor and then down regulate it as a result
	- Lisuride- Hard to get 
	- Chromium, inositol and meditation, getting more sun generally, ideally cutting out bread, pasta, beer and anything else that irritates the digestive system
	- If general approach like melatonin and good sleep hygiene doesn't improve the sleep, then we can try to find a more specific culprit: this one for the serotonin 2A receptor (5HT2A)
	- Serotonin is often seenas the happy neurotransmitter but this is not always the case; overactive 5HT2A receptor and certain gene variants of the HTR2A have been associated in studies with many health condition
	- Make sure to check interactions and side effects of each supplements and med; trazodone may increase the desire to drink, so not great for people that are in recovery, this may also apply to other 5Th2A antagonists being used as sleep aid, so seroquel and cyproheptadine
