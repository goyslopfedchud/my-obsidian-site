## Hypothyroid or Hashimoto's
	- Avoid gluten, high PUFA oils, and goitrogenic foods
	- Light exercise routine you can stick to
	- Getting thyroid medication prescribed if necessary
	- Supplements to consider: selenium, low dose iodine, vitamin B1, zinc
## High cortisol or Cushing's
	- Avoid high PUFA oils, sugar, high glycemic foods, and alcohol. Gluten for Cushing's
	- Prioritizing sleep if possible
	- Supplements to consider: magnesium, zinc, phosphatidylserine, vitamin B1 & B5

## Additional info
	- A lot of people are unaware that they have hypothyroidism or high cortisol 
	- Feeling cold is a big sign of hypothyroidism or Hashimoto's: water retention and puffy type of weight gain, brain fog, tiredness, constipation, dry skin
	- High cortisol or Cushing's, round chubby face is common-moon face, fat abdomen but skinny arms and legs, stretch marks that are usually purple, high blood pressure, sleep quality terrible, (pseudoscience) might feel like people mistreat you for no good reason
	- Get tested so you don't waste time on the wrong culprit of the weight gain 
	- Hypothyroidism and Hashimoto symptoms include being sensitive to cold, brain fog, physical fatigue, dry skin, hair thinning and excess shedding, puffy face and water retention, possible issues with mood, sometimes it can mimic ADD / ADHD
	- A lot of the symptoms overlaps with dysautonomia and hypothalamic inflammation- With those, you usually get lightheaded, and have difficulty acclimatizing to both hot weather and cold weather
	- The labs you can do for hypothyroid and Hashimoto's are: standard blood test for T4, T3, and TSH, and make sure to push for APO antibody test and TgAb test
	- High cortisol and Cushing's syndrome, you can expect round face, fat abdomen but disproportionately skinny arms and legs, lower back is fat, muscle loss and inability to gain muscle, stretch marks that are purple, irritable, maybe waking up between 22:00 - 02:00: 24 hour salivary cortisol test or late night salivary cortisol test, and LDDST test
	- High cortisol should be easy enough to tell just by symptoms, but it's still good to get the tests done, you can always do the diet mentioned while you wait for the results
