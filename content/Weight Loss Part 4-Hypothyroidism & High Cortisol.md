Hypothyroid or Hashimoto's
	- Avoid gluten, high PUFA oils, and goitrogenic foods
	- Light exercise routine you can stick to
	- Getting thyroid medication prescribed if necessary
	- Supplements to consider: selenium, low dose iodine, vitamin B1, zinc
High cortisol or Cushing's
	- Avoid high PUFA oils, sugar, high glycemic foods, and alcohol. Gluten for Cushing's
	- Prioritizing sleep if possible
	- Supplements to consider: magnesium, zinc, phosphatidylserine, vitamin B1 & B5

Additional info
	- A lot of people are unaware that they have hypothyroidism or high cortisol 
	- Feeling cold is a big sign of hypothyroidism or Hashimoto's: water retention and puffy type of weight gain, brain fog, tiredness, constipation, dry skin
	- High cortisol or Cushing's, round chubby face is common-moon face, fat abdomen but skinny arms and legs, stretch marks that are usually purple, high blood pressure, sleep quality terrible, (pseudoscience) might feel like people mistreat you for no good reason
	- Get tested so you don't waste time on the wrong culprit of the weight gain
