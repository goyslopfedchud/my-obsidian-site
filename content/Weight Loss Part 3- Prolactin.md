## Antipsychotics
## What increases prolactin
	- PUFA, exorphin (gluten), casomorphine (dairy)
	- Antipsychotic (1st & 2nd gen), SSRI
	- Low dopamine, high serotonin 2A & estrogen
## Ways to lower prolactin
	- Vitamin E 200 - 400 IU (134 - 268 mg)
	- Vitamin B6 10 - 20 mg (Pyridoxine HCI)
	- Zinc 5 - 25 mg
	- Chaste berry
	- Ginseng
	- Aspirin
	- Cabergoline 

## Additional info
	- Prolactin is a different reason people can't lose wait despite caloric deficit
	- Symptom wise: low motivation, more sensitive to stress, low libido, daytime sleepiness, and increased hunger
	- Lactation in men can cause enlarged breast
	- Hypothetically you can increase dopamine by taking stimulants to lower prolactin but when you're on antipsychotics (1st & 2nd gen), that'll overpower the stimulants, thus won't work: for this case you can try Vitamin E, B6, and zinc 
	- Dopamine is a natural antagonist to prolactin, so raising dopamine makes sense in most cases, but 1st & 2nd generation antipsychotics will usually overpower dopamine agonists, so that's where Vitamin E, B6, and zinc might be useful- You may also need to control the blood glucose
	- Vitamin B6 can eventually become toxic for some people, so biotin 1,000 mcg can be used as a prophylactic
	- If you decide to go higher on the B6 dose then increase biotin and consider adding Vitamin B5
	- Prolactin is an important hormone like any other, it has the effect of making you more nurturing, both for men and women: it helps you eat more during pregnancy
