## What increases prolactin
	- PUFA, exorphin ( gluten), casomorphine ( dairy)
	- Antipsychotic ( 1st & 2nd gen), SSRI
	- Low dopamine, high serotonin 2A & estrogen
## Ways to lower prolactin
	- Vitamin E 200 - 400 IU(134 - 268 mg)
	- Vitamin B6 10 - 20 mg ( Pyridoxine HCI)
	- Zinc 5 - 25 mg
	- Chaste berry
	- Ginseng
	- Aspirin
	- Cabergoline 

## Additional info
	- Prolactin is a different reason people can't lose wait despite caloric deficit
	- Symptom wise: low motivation, more sensitive to stress, low libido, daytime sleepiness, and increased hunger
	- Lactation in men can cause enlarged breast
	- Hypothetically you can increase dopamine by taking stimulants to lower prolactin but when you're on antipsychotics ( 1st & 2nd gen), that'll overpower the stimulants, thus won't work: for this case you can try Vitamin E, B6, and zinc
