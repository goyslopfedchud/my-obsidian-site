## Additional info
	- If depression or tiredness is caused by the deficiency then taking B12 shots can relieve them, almost instantly
	- If there is post acute withdrawal from stimulants or alcohol and there's fatigue and depression from that, then B12 and B1 would be the first ones that would be recommended
	- If you need way too many rest days between workouts to restore strength, then you would look at B12 and B1 again 
	- Anything that truly tires out the nervous system including long periods of great stress 
	- If you're very deficient then taking B12 can cause initial shock reaction- Feeling overstimulated usually and being irritable for a couple hours 
	- Could ask your doctor for hydroxocobalamin shot, if they won't do it then you can get a slow dissolve lozenge of hydroxocobalamin; leave it in your cheek for about an hour
