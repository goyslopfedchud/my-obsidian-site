## Foods that may sap energy and disrupt metabolism
	- Gluten
	- Dairy
	- High lectin foods (whole grains, nuts, beans, legumes)
	- High oxalate foods (spinach, kale, celery)
	- Industrial vegetable oils
	- Alcohol
## Supplements
	- Vitamin B1 50 - 200 mg
	- L-Carnitine 250 - 500 mg
	- Molybdenum 100 - 250 mcg
	- Pycnogenol (pine bark extract)
	- NADh 2.5 - 5 mg
	- D-Phenylalanine 500 - 1,000 mg

## Big Pharma
	- Low dose naltrexone (LDN) 0.25 - 4.5 mg
	- Bromantane 50 - 200 mg
