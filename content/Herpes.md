## 8 human herpes viruses
	- HSV-1 (cold sore), ~67% of people under 50
	- HSV-2 (genital herpes), ~13% of people between age 15 - 49
	- VZV (Shingles, Chicken pox), ~90+%
	- Epstein Barr Virus (Mono), ~90 - 95%
	- Cytomegalovirus, 40 - 50% in developed countries
	- HHV-6, 80 - 96% of adults
	- HHV-7, 75 - 98% of adults
	- HHV-8, prevalence heavily depends on region
## What my cause more breakouts
	- Nuts (almonds, peanuts, walnuts, cashew)
	- Seeds (sesami, hemp, sunflower, pupkin)
	- Gelatin, hemp protein, pea protein
	- Soy products (tofu, saitan, tempeh, natto)
## Little bit safer but still caution
	- Oats, whole wheat, whole grains
	- Coconut (flour, protein, meat)
	- Dark chocolates, cacao
	- Hot spices (cayenne, chili, ghost)
	- Citrus foods (tomato, lemon)
## Others
	- Stress, excess sunlight
