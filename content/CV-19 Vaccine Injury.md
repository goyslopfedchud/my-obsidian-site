## Chronic fatigue, POTS
	1. Vitamin B1 25 - 500 mg
	2. Lactoferrin
	3. Molybdenum 100 - 250 mcg
	- This seems to be the most common complaint; start vitamin B1 at 25 mg, slowly building dose up to 500 mg a day, then lower it around 100 mg a day; as the B1 dose increases, you may need to add vitamin B2, about half the dose of vitamin B1
## Myocarditis, Pericarditis

	2. Get sufficient sleep
	3. Stimulant & alcohol avoidance
	4. Low PUFA diet
	5. Magnesium
	6. Coenzyme Q10
	- Chest pain, heart issues; get that checked out, whether you have symptoms or not

## Neuropsychiatric symptoms

	2. Niacin 100 mg - 300 mg
	3. Vitamin B1 100 - 300 mg
	4. Lactoferrin
	5. Molybdenum 100 - 250 mcg

	7. N-acetylcysteine 600 - 1,200 mg
