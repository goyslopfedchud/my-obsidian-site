## Variations
	- Zopiclone (Imovane) 5 hours half life
	- Eszopiclone (Lunesta) 6 hours
	- Zolpidem (Ambien) 2.5 hours
	- Zaleplon (Sonata) 1 hour
## Side effects
	- Memory black out
	- Sleep walking
	- Physical & psychological dependency
	- Zopliclone & eszopiclone: metallic taste, hallucination, euphoria, increased cravings for nicotine & other substances
	- Zolpidem: nausea and vomitting
## Day after effects
	- Fatigue
	- Irritability
	- Mood swings
	- Depression
	- Anxiety
	- Rebound insomnia
