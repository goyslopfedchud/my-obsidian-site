## What may increase neuropathic pains
	- vasoldilator supplements: Arginine, Niacin, NAC, Pycnogenol, Lions Mane, Beet Root
	- long hot baths, alcohol, dehydration
	- insulin resistance, systemic herpes, FM, GBS, Lyme, MS, toxicity (mercury, B6)
	- gluten, high histamine, lectin, and oxalate foods
## What may help
	- benfotiamine 300 - 900 mg
	- palmitoylethanolamide 300 - 600 mg
	- magnesium 100 - 300 mg (vasoldilator)
	- alpha lipoic acid 300 - 600 mg (vasoldilator)
	- hydroxocobalamin 250 mg

## Additional info
	- with nerupathic pains you might want to avoid vasodilators which can facilitate healing but also cause pains at the same time; so as long as there is ongoing neuro degeneration it's going to be one step forward one step back for no reason 
	- histamine is a strong vasodilator
	- gluten is more so known for msucle pains and join pains, but it can also do cold and painful fingers or sharp shooting chest pains and random pains anywhere in the body
	- same with high lectin foods but just to a lesser extent 
	- oxalate is more so mechanical inflammation, although it can also do neuroinflammation 
	- neuropathy has different culprits, so none of the supplements listed here is universal
	- it's pretty rough because it's not something you can jsut soldier through with high pain tolerance
	- a lot of times the painkillers don't work that well
