## Symptoms associated with candida
	- Brain fog, poor memory
	- Intense cravings for sweets, simple carbs and alcohol
	- Chronic sinus or ear infections
	- Endometriosis, PMS, yeast infections
	- Frequent bloating & gas, IBD, colitis, constipation or diarrhea
	- Fatigue, mood swings, anxiety
	- Oral thrush, white tongue
## What increase candida
	- Overuse of antibiotics, antacids 
	- Oral contraceptives, copper IUD
	- High sugar, high starch diet, junk foods
	- Alcohol, especially beer and wine
## Die off symptoms (Herxheimer's)
	- Fly like feeling
	- Skin reactions

## Additional info
	- If you were to kill them too fast you get a die off reaction called Herxheimer's 
	- You have to understand the parasite to host relationship here if you were to do antifungals or deworming 
	- If you try to kill off the parasite too fast, if you try to nuke them, that'll end up killing the good guys too and you have all this parasite waste to clean up- in this case which is Herxheimer's
	- Another way to look at this: think of people or situations in your life that are energetic parasite to you, so you want to stop creating a hospitable environment for them- in this case it's junk foods, or else they'll keep coming back or something else will take their place
	- All this is just a long way of saying: don't go on a detox spree right away because that might cause more problems
