## Lifestyle changes
	- More sunlight exposure, get shower filter
	- Eliminate high fructose fruits, HFCS, honey, and refined sugar
	- Avoid gluten and alcohol if possible
## Other triggers
	- Dexamethasone
	- Lithium
	- Buspirone
	- Some antipsychotics and antibiotics
	- Iron supplements
## Supplemetns that may help
	- Niacin or Niacinamide 50 - 250 mg
	- Riboflavin 10 - 50 mg
	- Biotin 500 - 3,000 mcg
	- EGCG 200 mg, or lactoferrin
	- Zinc 5 - 12.5 mg
	- Probiotics
