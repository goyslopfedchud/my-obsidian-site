## Additional info
	- With autism there are several thing we can look at 
	- Pinpointing the cause and addressing that could make a drastic difference
	- Speech delays or being nonverbal: you may first look into folate receptor autoantibodies
	- Trouble reading social cues, that could be low GABA, high glutamate 
	- Behavioral issues: OCD, ODD, tics- Look at PANS, PANDAS; it's highly relevant but it's just really hard to find specialist for 
	- If there's complaints of chronic pains or let's say frequent urination then you may look at oxalates 
	- Observing people with developmental issues, it's very common for them to have digestive issues and bloated stomach, so you could look at food sensitivities, and immediately cut out gluten, grains, dairy, processed foods
