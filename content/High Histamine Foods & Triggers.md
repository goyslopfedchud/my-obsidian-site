
## List
	- Meat or seafood that's been dried, aged, cured, canned, or left out of the fridge too long or thawed slowly (bacon, ham, dried fish, sardiness, smoked salmon)
	- Dairy (yogurt, kefir, blue or aged cheese)
	- Overly ripe banana & avocado, chocolates, vinegar
	- Fermented foods (sauerkraut, kimchi, kombucha, natto tempeh, mist), pickled veggies
	- Dried fruits (raisin, fig, dates, prunes)
	- Cashews, brasil nuts, peanuts, walnut almonds
	- Seeds (sesame, pumpkin, sunflower)
	- Citrus fruits (lemon, lime, grapefruit), cayenne & chili
	- Alcohol (especially red wine and beet), green tea
## Avoid if very sensitive, but genrally okay
	- Tomato
	- Strawberry
	- Pineapple
	- Kiwi
	- Papaya
## Supplements to help
	- Vitamin C
	- Quercetin
	- Bromelain
	- DAO Enzyme
	- Stinging Nettle Extract
	- Black seed oil

## Additional info
	- Meat or seafood that's been cured, dried canned or smoked, gets really high in histamine 
	- Yogurt might be okay depending on how sensitive you are 
	- Nuts and seeds are probably okay as well, or you can switch to macadamia nuts
	- Everyone has their limit of how much histamine they can tolerate; for some people it's seemingly infinite, whereas if you have mast cell issues then very little can trigger itchiness or brain fog
	- If you were to consume something high in histamine, take quercetin, vitamin C, and bromelain like 30 minutes before the meal
