## What may raise risk for some
	- Sleep deprivation, excess screen time, fever, hypoglycemia, high blood pressure, infections
	- Withdrawal from benzos / alcohol / gabapentin
	- Some stimulants / antibiotics / antihistamines / antipsychotics
	- MSG, aspartame, gluten, excess sugar, high PUFA diet
## What may help for some
	- Ketogenic or whole foods diet (clean version)
	- L-Taurine 1,500 - 5,000 mg
	- Magnesium 100 - 400 mg
	- Vitamin B6 10 - 50 mg
## Others
	- Black seed oil
	- Saffron
	- Omega 3
## Big Pharma 
	- Levetiracetam
	- Topiramate
	- Valproates
## Possible causes and key tests
	- Autoimmune encephalitis - CSF + antibody panel
	- Infectious encephalitis - CSF PCR +MRI
	- Biotin - thiamine disorder - MRI + SLC19A3
	- GLUT1 deficiency - CSF glucose + SLC2A1
	- B6-dependent epilepsy - Urine α-AASA + ALDH7A1
	- Cerebral folate deficiency - CSF 5-MTHF + FOLR1
	- Mitochondrial disease - Lactate + genetic panel
	- Low calcium / hypoparathyroidism - Calcium + PTH
## Additional info
	- Make sure to talk to the doctor before trying any of these because seizure is pretty dangerous
	- Ketogenic diet is looking quite attractive especially for medication resistant seizures
	- You don't have to have celiac or gluten intolerance to get neuroinflammation from it; whereas MSG and aspartame is more so theoretical and anecdotal but it's worth being on the list
	- Preferably ketogenic diet, if not then whole foods diet 
	- Considering the seriousness of seizures it wouldn't be an overkill at all to do kito or whole foods 
	- Not all seizure is from the same cause and those culprits usually don't show up on a blood test- find a doctor that is willing to give extensive tests
