## What disrupts endorphin & opioid receptors
	- Endorphin like peptides in foods (gluten, dairy, soy, spinach)
	- Refined sugar
## What may help with withdrawal
	- Buprenorphine 500 mcg
	- Sodium Ascorbate 30,000 mg + Molybdenum 250 mcg + loperamide
## Long term
	- Sunlight
	- Memenatine 2.5 mg
	- Low dose naltrexone 0.25 - 5 mg

## Additional info
	- The vitamin C (sodium ascorbate) may not work so well
	- Suboxone is not a bad option compared to relapses
	- There are some doctors that will push high doses like their livelihood depends on it, but most people can safely taper down to 2 mg a day: preferably closer to 500 mcg but not relapsing is more important and the lower doses will ensure your teeth don't melt
