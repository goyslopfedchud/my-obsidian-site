## Lifestyle changes
	- Take days off if you can, get more sunlight, abdomen stretches, yoga, meditation
	- Reducing processed foods, seed oils, and sugar
	- Avoid aged cheese, wine, overly ripe banana & avocado, cured or processed meat and fish (Migraines)

## What might help
	- Magnesium 50 - 100 mg, 2 - 3 times
	- Zinc 10 - 30 mg, 1 to 4 days prior to menses
	- Omega 3
	- Vitamin B1 (Thiamine HCI) 100 mg
	- Aspirin

## Root, Tea, Herb, Spice, Supplement
	- Raspberry leaf, Ginger, Cinnamon, Peppermint, Fennel

## Additional info
	- Cramps, heavy bleeding, tiredness, mood disturbances, bloating, migraines are symptoms
	- Not for premenstrual syndrome that goes away after your period actually starts, menstruations and PMS are two different events
	- Most of things in the list is to reduce the pain, a lot of them lowering prostaglandin, which might be efficient for fatigue
	- Too much ginseng can make the bleeding worse, although it's good for the fatigue: only do it half of serving once a day only during period, since it works right away
