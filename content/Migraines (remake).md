## What may increase migraines
	- Aged or moldy cheese
	- Overly ripe banana & avocado
	- Cured meats (bacon, ham, sausage, beef, jerky, salami)
	- Aged, canned, or smoked meat & fish
	- Alcohol (especially red wine)
	- Fermented foods (natto, tempeh yogurt, kimchi, sauerkraut, miso, soy sauce)
	- Cacao & dark chocolate
	- MSG & aspartame
	- 5-HTP, SAMe, excess B6 or D3, calcium
	- Selegiline, SSRI & SNRI, some contraceptives
## What may help
	- Meditation, exercise, regular sleep
	- Staying hydrated
	- Reduce time on screen, switch to non flickering lights
## Supplements 
	- Riboflavin 25 - 400 mg
	- Magnesium 100 - 400 mg
	- Coenzyme Q10 100 - 300 mg
	- PEA 300 - 600 mg
	- Zinc 5 - 15 mg
## Others
	- Omega 3
	- Molybdenum 250 mcg
	- Feverfew
	- DAO enzyme
	- Biotin
## Big Pharma
	- Ergotamines
	- Lisuride
	- Triptans
	- Aspirin
	- Gepants

## Additional info
	- People with migraines in most cases might tend to crave foods with above the list; but if you can try to be reasonable with the amount then there's a way around it 
	- With migraines we're looking at tyramine, histamine, glutamate, serotonin 2A receptor, estrogen, and sometimes nerve damage
	- If it's estrogen based, you want to look at molybdenum, zinc, and PEA
	- If it's nerve damage then biotin, PEA
	- Say there's a coinciding psychiatric stuff then you might just assume it's serotonin based migraines; so riboflavin and feverfew and meditation 
	- These are just to give you clues, not medical advice 
	- If it's histamine based like itchiness and runny nose, then you'd look at DAO enzymes and reducing a lot of the food items 
	- If CGRP antagonists work for you then Coenzyme Q10 also has that effect 
	- Magnesium is quite versatile here
