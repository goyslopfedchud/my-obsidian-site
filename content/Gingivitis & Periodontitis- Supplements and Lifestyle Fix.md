## What Aggravates Gingivitis & Periodontitis
	1. Alcohol
	2. Refined sugar
	3. Dry mouth
	4. Snoring, sleep apnea
	5. Too much systemic iron
	6. Smoking and chewing
	7. Food particles stuck in the gum
	8. Aggressive toothpick use
	9. Autoimmunity / lack of sunlight
	10. House dog with skin conditions
	11. Chronic oxalate poisoning
	12. High oxalate foods:
		- Raspberries
		- Dark chocolate & cocoa powder
		- Swiss chard
		- Buckwheat
		- Tofu, miso & other soya foods
		- Spinach
		- Potatoes & yams
		- Wheat bran
		- Black tea
		- Beans
		- Rhubard
		- Nuts & seeds
		- Beets
	- Unfortunately alcohol is the worst offender here
	- Refined sugar is not that bad
	- If you take medication that causes dry mouth especially when you're sleeping, not great
	- Not having high oxalate foods as staples because they can build up in your system, end up causing a lot of problems later on
	- Gingivitis and periodontitis is bacteria and autoimmune based, meaning they feed on iron- So not taking iron pills 
	- Smoking is probably not such a big deal- It's not like gingivitis will be cured by quitting smoking, it's more of a contributing factor
	- If the house pets like dogs smells bad, it might contribute, licking your hands and you're using your hand to touch your face etc, this is only possibility, might, and is unsure
## Deficiencies to Correct
	1. Vitamin C 1,000 - 3,000 mg
	2. Vitamin K2 100 mcg
	3. Vitamin B3 50 - 250 mg
	- Prioritize these three
	- Doesn't matter what form of C you take, it can be ascorbic acid or sodium ascorbate and if you need to take a lot of, then it's better to do with sodium ascorbate
	- As long as it's K2, it's fine 
	- If you don't like the flushing of B3, take niacinamide instead of niacin

## Other deficiencies
	1. Vitamin B9
	2. Selenium
	3. Coenzyme Q10
	4. Zinc (10 - 15 mg)
	- If coenzyme Q10 is what you already have then it can be used in place of vitamin K2
	- Periodontitis has a very distinct smell; you can sometimes smell on people but a lot of them don't really recognize, they have blindness to their own scent or their sense of smell is blocked- In that case is probably zinc deficiency
	- If you can't smell and the sense of smell is weak or blocked, you need zinc and quercetin 
	- If you're ever on iron pills or did some iron infusions, using iron cooking pans for extended periods of time, eating a lot of steak or red meat (which is fine), but having too much systemic iron; in order to process that you can take some lactoferrin or EGCG
	- If your hair is thinning or your nails are brittle, try vitamin B9 as in folinic acid or selenium
	- If all else fails or you're just reluctant to improve lifestyle for gingivitis and periodontitis, then you can try probiotics
	- S. Salivarius in the chewable or like candy form
	- Vitamin C, K2, B3, with oral tablet of salivarius, other probiotics in swallowing tablets
	- In about a month you should see some improvements
