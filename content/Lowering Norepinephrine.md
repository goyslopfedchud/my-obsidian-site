## Lifestyle changes 
	- Do cardio, reduce stress where possible
	- Address hypoglycemia, reduce high histamine foods (if intolerant)
	- Reduce stimulants & alcohol intake
## What may raise it
	- Phenylalanine
	- Tyrosine
	- Yohimbine
	- Synephrine
	- Rhodiola
	- Ginseng
	- Copper
## Supplements that may lower or manage it
	- L-Taurine
	- Melatonin (right before bed)
	- Molybdenum Chelate (not glycinate)
	- EGCG (from green tea extract)
	- Sodium Bicarbonate (after exercise)
## Pharma
	- Beta blocker
	- Clonidine
	- Guanfacine

## Additional info
	- You want healthy levels of norepinephrine during the day but some people have it in excess, where they might take beta blockers to manage the symptoms 
	- PTSD is heavily involved with the release of norepinephrine- it can make you physically stronger but high levels of norepinephrine also makes you stutter and blank out, which is unproductive today because you have to solve things with words and not violence
	- High circulating levels of unusable copper is an issue- molybdenum can be a short term antidote for that
	- You might be unknowingly histamine intolerant so epinephrine is raised in response 
	- People with histamine intolerance tend to have high anxiety 
	- If your blood sugar gets low, then you might get adrenaline dump- waking up at commonly around 03:00 
	- If you're taking MAO-A inhibitor then it's going to make norepinephrine linger
