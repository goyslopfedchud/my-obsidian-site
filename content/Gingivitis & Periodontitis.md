## Lifestyle Changes
	- Getting more sunlight
	- Avoid alcohol, junk food, and refined sugar
	- Reduce high oxalate foods (celery & spinach)
	- Addressing dry mouth, and snoring
	- Daily brushing & flossing to remove plaque
## Possible deficiencies
	- Vitamin C 1,000 - 2,000 mg
	- Vitamin K2 100 mcg or CoQ10 50 mg
	- Vitamin B3 50 -250 mg
## Other Supplements (pick up to 2)
	- Oral Probiotics
	- Quercetin or EGCg
	- Zinc 10 mg, Folinic acid 400 mcg, selenium 100 mcg, Astaxathin, or Lactoferrin

## Additional info
	- Gigivitis often goes unnoticed, it's a gum infection and inflammation, eventually leading to gum recession
	- Can happen from aggressive toothpick use, lots of smoking, house pet that has skin infection, poor oral hygiene, microbial health, things that causes mouth dry like alcohol, certain meds, sleep apnea, snoring
	- Can be related to poor kidney health, excess circulating iron-no iron pills
	- Start with correcting deficiencies and then oral probiotics
	- You can use toothpastes meant for periodontitis, making sure it's fluoride free
	- You will want to learn good brushing and flossing technique to remove the plaque, preferably soft toothbrushes
	- Vitamin B2, K2, and B3 you can do even lower doses, Vitamin K and CoQ10 can cause insomnia for some people
	- If you have loss of smell and taste, it could be due to low zinc
	- A lot of people have difficulty recognizing their bad breath, so you can combine zinc + Quercetin or EGCg or Lactoferrin (whichever you can tolerate)
	- S. Salivarius as oral probiotics, other options are: L. Reuteri, L Brevis, and B. Subtulis
	- Mouth taping might be a quick temporary solution for dry mouth during sleep
	- The house pet thing is a theory, but if they smell bad, try to feed them a cleaner diet, and don't let them lick your face
