
## General changes
	- Avoid sugar and sweet liquor
	- Sleep at least 7 hours after drinking
	- Don't neglect hydration
	- Try not to do stimulants while drinking
	- Don't mix with opioids or benzos
## Supplements
	- Vitamin C 500 - 1,000 mg
	- Activated charcoal
	- Magnesium 50 - 100 mg
	- Vitamin B1 100 mg
	- NAC 500 - 1,200 mg
	- Circumin (Theracumin of Longvida)
	- Dihyromyricetin

## Additional info
	- If it's moderate drinking then Vitamin C and charcoal are good enough 
	- If you drink heavily then it's reasonable to get all the supplements then you can stay at least functional and not wreck your body
	- NAC and circumin can have sobering effect, not to the extent of doing a bump (powder of cocaine etc) or anything but it kills the buzz, might want to take them later; but if you drink compulsively, they can help with that- So you can take them earlier
	- When you're closed to blacking out and you gotta go to bed soon, pop charcoal, NAC, or curcumin and sleep for 7+ hours
	- Don't take everything at once, splitting everything up according to their efficacy and the cross interactions
	- Aim to have a mid sized glass of water for every drink you have
	- Vitami C and activated charcoal with your first drink, and then every two hours; too much C gives you the runs
	- B1 with the first drink, magnesium near the end of the night
	- Other to consider:
	- Sulforaphane
	- Folinic Acid
	- Alpha Lipoic Acid
