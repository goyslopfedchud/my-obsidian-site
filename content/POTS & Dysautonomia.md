## Lifestyle changes
	- Getting more sunlight, hydration, and more salt
	- Reduce high histamine foods (cure or aged meat, some seafood, fermented foods, overly ripe banana & avocado)
	- Avoid gluten and processed foods if possible
## What to avoid
	- Long hot baths
	- Exerting beyond capacity
	- Overdoiing iron or B6 supplements 
## What may help
	- Vitamin B1 50 - 100 mg
	- Vitamin B5 100 - 500 mg
	- Lactoferrin 250 mg (as needed)
	- Molybdenum 100 - 250 mcg (as needed)
	- Butyrate (not if you have herpes)

## Additional info
	- Many variations of root cause like infection, autonomic nerve damage, iron toxicity, bad batch of shots- Unreasonably common though
	- Recommend trying to get a proper diagnosis and see if you can get on disability benefits while you heal 
	- Main interest is titrating B1 and B5 slowly, doing lactoferrin and molybdenum as needed, like eventually as needed
	- A mass mono treatment such as 1,800 mg of B1 will eventually stop working: have a formula to take care of different things from different angles
	- Start slow with B1 and B5, B1 can cause initial shock reaction
	- Molybdenum may help if there's anxiety, irritability, or itchy legs but don't take high amounts like 3 mg as suggested with some protocols
	- High circulating iron is an issue for unstable blood pressure, so it is advised against taking iron pills, rather improve iron metabolism with lactoferrin
