## What disrupts the thyroid
	- Goitrogens (cruciferous vegetables, soy)
	- Gluten
	- High PUFA ois ( aka seed oils)
	- High oxalate foods ( spinach, kale, celery)
	- Halogen toxicity: fluorides & bromides
	- L-Carnitine or high does vitamin C
## Supplements that may help
	- Selenium 50 - 200 mcg
	- Zinc 5 - 12.5 mg
	- Vitamin B1 50 - 200 mg
	- Lactobacillus Reuteri (with caution)
	- Iodine 50 - 200 mcg (risky for Hashimoto's)
	- L-Tyrosine 200 - 2,000 mg ( with caution)
