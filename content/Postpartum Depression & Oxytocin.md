## What may worsen PPD
	- PTSD & chronic stress, lack of sleep
	- Synthetic folic acid
	- High dopamine spikes lifestyle
## What may help
	- Whole food diet
	- Get more sunlight
	- Methyfolate or folinic acid 200 - 400 mcg
	- Selenium, omega 3
## Oxytocin
