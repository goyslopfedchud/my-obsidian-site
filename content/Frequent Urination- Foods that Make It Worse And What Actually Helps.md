
## Factors Associated with Frequent Urination
	1. Dysautonomia (POTS)
	2. Low vasopressin
	3. Insulin resistance
	4. Oxalate toxicity
	5. Copper toxicity
	6. Iron toxicity
	7. Nerve damage
	8. Basal ganglia
	9. Food sensitivity
	10. Histamine intolerance
	11. Cortisol too low
	12. Electrolyte imbalance
	13. Pelvic floor issues
	14. Inflamed prostate

## Supplements
	1. Lactoferrin
	2. Vitamin B1 50 - 100 mg / day
	3. Vitamin B5 100 - 500 mg / day
	4. Quercetin
	5. Zinc 5 - 15 mg / day
	6. Molybdenum 100 - 250 mcg / day
	7. Pumpkin seed extract
	8. Cannabis
	9. More sodium or potassium
	- Lactoferrin is good for general interstitial cystitis and UTI, might be because it helps to escort out the excess iron in the blood; high iron symptoms are, brain fog, cold hands & feet, getting lightheaded often, tiredness
	- Vitamin B1 if you have dysautoniomia symptoms; lightheadedness, tiredness, exercise intolerance, vitamin B5 as well, or if you feel angry often for no reason, have difficulty sleeping 
	- Quercetin, if you have histamine intolerance 
	- It's very rare for someone to have only frequent urination and no other symptoms 
	- Low zinc would be loss of smell, taste, appetite, maybe depression
	- Since zinc chelates copper, you'll want to take molybdenum with it
	- If you suspect copper or B6 toxicity; getting anxious, irritable, tired; then molybdenum might help with those plus the frequent urination 
	- If you have offending foods from the previous list, then cannabis seems to calm the nerves of the bladder
	- Sometimes you just need more sodium or potassium 

## Lifestyle fixes
	1. Lower carbohydrate intake
	2. Gluten avoidance
	3. Avoid sweets, and artificial sweeteners
	4. Reducing oxalate intake gradually
	5. Reduce histamine intake
	6. Eliminate problematic supplements
