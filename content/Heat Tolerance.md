## Lifestyle changes
	- Stay hydrated, avoid alcohol
	- Reduce histamine intake (cured and processed meat & seafood, fermented foods)
	- Take care of gut health, reduce inflammation
	- Reduce high PUFA oils
## What may help
	- Watermelon, coconut water, and enough salt
	- Vitamin B1 50 - 100 mg
	- Vitamin C 200 - 500 mg
	- Bacillus subtilis
	- Magnesium 50 - 100 mg 2x day
## Others
	- CoQ10
	- Selenium
	- Vitamin E 

## Additional info
	- You could have heat intolerance for number of reasons; could be POTS, menopause, hyperthyroid, high circulating blood copper 
	- In any way we want to stay hydrated and drink less alcohol, reduce high histamine foods just for the summer
	- Part of your brain called hypothalamus, different from thyroid, works as a thermostat and regulate blood pressure
	- When hypothalamus is inflamed you have trouble regulating temperature- Maybe it was the case from any of heat stroke patients 
	- Don't try to push your limits with heat with these supplements, since they are mostly based on animal studies 
	- It's a lot easier to make you tolerate cold better then heat 
	- Vitamin B1 greatly helps during the summer
