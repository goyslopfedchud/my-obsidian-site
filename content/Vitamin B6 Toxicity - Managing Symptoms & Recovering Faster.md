## Symptoms
	1. Tingling, electric burning, and numbness mostly in the peripheral nerves
	2. Insomnia
	3. Pain near the temple
	4. Fatigue & POTS
	5. Poor balance, clumsy
	6. Cold extremities
	7. Frequent urination, sensitive bladder
	8. Brain fog, reduced cognition & concentration
	9. Increased histamine intolerance
	10. Anxiety, panic, agoraphobia
	11. Tinnitus
	12. Psychosis, delusion, confusion
	13. Light sensitivity visual snow
	- If you have more than 5 of the symptoms, then that's a cause for concern
	- It mostly resembles peripheral neuropathy, diabetes, autonomic nervous system dysfunction
	- It seems that for most people it takes about 6 months to a year to heal completely
## Mechanisms & Theories
	1. B6 mobilizes deep tissue copper, mercury, and iron
	2. Oxalate dumping
	3. Standard American diet
	4. Methylation issue
	5. Over supplementing thiamine disulfides
	6. Deficiencies of B6 processing enzymes
	7. Increased demand for B7 & Molybdenum
	8. Past GABAergic drug use causing B6 retention: Alcohol, phenibut,, benzos, barbiturates
	9. Sluggish liver
	10. Aldehyde and sulfite toxicity
	- B6 mobilizing deep tissue heavy metals can cause neuropathic symptoms 
	- If people say they got B6 toxicity from eating high B6 foods, it's questionable that their pathways to metabolizing B6 was just messed up, or it could be that they were eating high oxalate foods, as B6 causes oxalate dumping, which then can cause neuropathic symptoms 
	- Oxalate can build up in your system throughout the years of eating high oxalate foods
	- Methylation is an important part in metabolizing B6; think of standard American diet with the folic acid enriched foods, if you're genetically incompatible to folic acids then that can mess up your methlation cycle, thus messing up the B6 metabolism
	- These are just theories why B6 toxicity happens

## Managing Symptoms
	1. Biotin 20 - 100 mg (not micrograms)
	2. B5 20 - 100 mg
	3. Molybdenum 20 - 100 mcg
	4. Low histamine diet
	5. Avoid vasodilators
	6. Stay hydrated
	7. B1, Caffeine, ginseng, yohimbine (blood pressure)
	8. Gabapentin or pregbalin (nerve pain)
	9. PEA (nerve pain)
	10. Benfotiamine (nerve pain)
	11. CBD (neuroinflammation)
	12. Aspirin (migraine, headache)
	13. NSAIDs (neuroinflammation)
	- The biotin, B5, and molybdenum, they all work synergistically to utilize B6 as well as relieving the symptoms of B6 toxicity
	- You probably will want to go on a low histamine diet because histamine can aggravate the neuropathic symptoms 
	- Avoid anything that dilates blood vessels like hot bath and a lot of supplements
	- Vassel dilation will eventually be important for neuroregeneration and recovery; but in the beginning stages you'll probably want to avoid them just for comfort
	- The top 6 are the most important ones you want to start with
	- Biotin, B5, and molybdenum will most likely relieve you of the neuropathic symptoms 
	- If you get POTS or dysotonomia symptoms like low blood pressure then you can try B1, caffeine, ginseng, or yohimbine when you need them
	- If your neuropathic symptoms are not relieved by the 3 combinations, then you can try things like gabapentin and PEAs, as well as benfotiamine and CBD, aspirin or ibuprofen
## How to Recover Faster
## B6 Metabolism
	1. Biotin 20 - 100 mg
	2. B5 20 - 100 mg
	3. Molybdenum 20 - 100 mcg
	4. Zinc 5 - 15 mg (alkaline phosphatase)
	5. Riboflavin (PNPO)
	6. Magnesium (PDXK)
	- If you don't want to do anything else than just do the top 3
## Autonomic Nervous System
	1. B1 or Benfotiamine
	2. B5
	3. B12 (Hydroxocobalamin)
	- 100 - 300 mg per day of thiamine hydrochloride or benfotiamine if there's symptoms of autonomic nervous system dysfunction, making sure it's not thiamine disulfides
	- If you did get nerve damage from B6 toxicity, you can eventually think about taking nutrients to recover from it; you might do this if the biotin, B5, and molybdenum didn't resolve the symptoms, with uncomfortable sypmtoms from trying to recover the nerves
	- Start this after a month of last dose of B6, after things have calmed down

## Additional info
	- The combination of biotin 50 milligrams, B5 50 milligrams, and molybdenum 50 micrograms should do the job for neuropathic pains, making sure you have the ratios right: if nerve damages haven't progressed far, then biotin, B5 & molybdenum will relieve most of the symptoms within few days
	- You may need to go on a low B6 diet for couple months
	- Getting Plasma PLP test might give a clear progress on recovery, and it might be difficult to get this test, depending on your location, doctor, or insurance, etc
	- If after 3 months, you still cannot tolerate high B6 foods, then B6 itself isn`t the issue
	- You may have overdone iron supplements in the past or have hemochromatosis
	- Systemic oxalate toxicity can cause B6 intolerance and taking thiamine disulfides can unmask already present B6 toxicity
