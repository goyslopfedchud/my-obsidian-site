## Things that worsen sleep
	- Poor sleep hygiene and stress
	- Alcohol, stimulants, some medications
## Sleep aids ( pick up to 3)
	- Melatonin 500 - 2,000 mcg, time release
	- Magnesium Threonate
	- Maryjuana ( if legal)
	- DORA ( lemborexant, suvorexant)
	- Others: Chamomile, L-Theanine, Glycine, Valerian, Lemon Balm, Diphenhydramine ( or quercetin)

## Additional info
	- Not all insomnia is the same; if it comes back, you might not be able to solve it with what had worked the last time. E.G. Trazodone and Ambien works for some people and doesn't for some
	- Some nights you just don't sleep well because of stress of bad sleep hygiene 
	- Melatonin is preferably time release; if it's immediate release, see if you do 500 mcg, this dose is less likely to cause bad dreams and next day drowsiness
	- Magnesium can paradoxically cause insomnia for some people; if you get frequent urination or agitation from mag, then that's a sign, but magnesium threonate is usually better tolerated
	- L-Theanine and Glycine can also paradoxically worsen sleep, though it's rare
	- Orexin antagonists, DORA, have less side effects compared to quetiapine and zolpidem, but it can cause sleep paralysis if you go over certain amount
	- Sleep hygiene practices:
	- Wake up at the same time everyday, and look at outdoor light first thing in the morning, go to bed at the same time
	- Slowly reduce indoor lighting during the evening, you can also use blue block glasses
	- Try to make your room as dark as possible, and on the colder side 18C is great
	- Avoid meals at least 3 hours before bed; tablespoon of honey is okay if you can tolerate that
	- Ideally stop working or being on your phone at least an hour before going to bed
	- There are more neurochemical specific cases where sleep hygiene does little to non
