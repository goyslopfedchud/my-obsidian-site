	- Avoid industrial vegetable oils
	- Reduce high glycemic foods
	- Eliminate processed foods & gluten if possible
	- Foods that may help: Fenugreek, flaxseed oil, maitake mushroom, cinnamon, licorice root

## Additional info
	- PCOS: weight gain in the abdomen, arms, back and chest, periods become irregular or just none, maybe heavy menstrual bleeding, fertility issues and absence of ovulation, development of hair growth in the face, chest and back, hair loss in the center of the head, receding hairline in the front and maybe the temple area, insomnia, mood swings, acne, oily skins, elevation of cytokines- Chronic inflammation and maybe autoimmunity
	- Insulin resistance might be a primary driver of PCOS
	- Might take years to find the right medications
	-Paleo diet: address the root cause- No process foods, no grains, sounds hard but might tget the job done much more quickly 
	- Now called Polyendocrine Metabolic Ovary Syndrome (PMOS)
	- The main symptoms are hirsutism, so it would look like women gaining hair in places where men would; facial hair, back and chest hair, and oily skin and acne
	- Inability to become pregnant, period changes, heavy menstrual bleeding, low libido, no ovulation
	- Weight gain, characterized as apple shaped- Fat accumulation in the chest, arms, belly, and back
	- Insulin resistance and high cortisol may be the main driver of PCOS
